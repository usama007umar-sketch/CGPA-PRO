Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about using CGPA Pro.

How is CGPA calculated?

CGPA is total quality points divided by total credit hours. Quality points for each course equal credit hours multiplied by grade point.

Can I add more subjects?

Yes. Click “Add Subject” to add as many course rows as you need.

Can I remove a subject?

Yes. Use the Remove button. The result recalculates automatically.

Is my data saved online?

No. This version performs calculations directly in your browser and does not send course data to a server.
